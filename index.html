Frequently asked

How do I scan my Mac?

On the first screen, choose “Scan my whole Mac” or “Pick a folder”, then grant access in the macOS panel. Spaceback can only read folders you explicitly allow — it’s a sandboxed App Store app.

Where do deleted files go?

Every removal moves the file to the Trash, so nothing is ever permanently lost until you empty it yourself. You can restore anything at any time.

What does Pro unlock?

A one-time purchase (no subscription, ever) that adds exact-duplicate cleanup, photo-clutter cleanup (look-alikes & screenshots), whole-Mac scans and automation. Free already includes large & old files, Downloads cleanup, the storage map and Trash.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new Mac?

Open Settings inside Spaceback and tap “Restore”, or “Restore Purchases” on the unlock screen. It re-checks your App Store account and restores Pro instantly.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. Everything runs on your Mac — no account, no network, no analytics, no tracking. See our Privacy Policy.
